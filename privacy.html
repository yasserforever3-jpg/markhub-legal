MarkHub — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MarkHub may collect and use information when you interact with the MarkHub Discord server, bot, and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Discord server roles
  • Messages or information submitted through MarkHub commands and tickets
  • Order-related information
  • Information necessary to provide or manage MarkHub services

We only aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate and improve our services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and manage MarkHub services.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Assign or manage Discord roles.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the MarkHub Discord server and bot.
  • Improve our services and user experience.

3. Information Sharing

MarkHub does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with MarkHub staff or service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the services, process an order, provide support, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order management, support, security, and record keeping.

5. Discord

MarkHub operates through Discord. Your interaction with our server and bot is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. MarkHub does not control how Discord independently collects or processes your information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by MarkHub from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

If you have questions about information associated with your MarkHub account or would like to request deletion of information that MarkHub controls, contact the MarkHub administration team through the official MarkHub Discord server. Some information may need to be retained when required for security, legal, financial, or operational reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

MarkHub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The updated version will include a new "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the MarkHub administration team through the official MarkHub Discord server.
